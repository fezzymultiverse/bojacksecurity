# Privacy
Files and notes stay on the phone, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. No accounts, analytics or servers; nothing is uploaded.
Plain text: the folder a file is in, app settings, the failed-attempt counter.
Restored files are ordinary decrypted copies in Documents/BojackSecures/Restored. Auto-delete is available in Settings.
Sync folder and backup file contain only encrypted data and need your original password.
Biometrics keep the password in Android Keystore. Feedback opens your email app; nothing is sent until you send it.
No password recovery.

Sharing: when you share an item, the app builds an encrypted copy and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). That app and its servers see only the encrypted file, never the password. Temporary copies in the app cache are deleted after 10 minutes.
